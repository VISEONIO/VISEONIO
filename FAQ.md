# What does VISEON do?

**Be understood. By everyone, and everything.**

VISEON helps people and AI understand your organisation correctly, across your website, enterprise systems and analytics tools.

We build and maintain a **digital twin of your organisation — your primary catalogue of concepts, knowledge and relationships**. It gives what matters to your business a clear, canonical definition that people, systems and AI can share. Built on open infrastructure and standards, it’s addressable at the edge for fast access at scale.

VISEON is your **semantic control plane — think of it as an operating system for AI**. It governs what things mean, which sources to trust, and which rules AI should follow when answering questions or helping people act.

Visitors explore your knowledge through VISEON Ask; compatible AI agents connect through MCP. Together, these help people and agents **discover, discuss and transact** with your organisation.

Start with a business challenge, or try our free Semantic Entity Assessment of your website. We’ll build from there.
